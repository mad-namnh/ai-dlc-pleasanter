# REQ-010 — 総務課長 reads all contracts

- **Status:** awaiting G1
- **Source:** `sources/2026-11-11-interview-vendor-contract-management.md` — A6
- **Type (EARS):** ubiquitous

## Requirement

The system shall allow the 総務課長 to read every contract.

## Acceptance criteria

1. As 総務課長, the contract list contains contracts of every 担当部門 and in every state.
2. As 総務課長, a single contract of any 担当部門 can be opened with all its items and its PDF file.

## Notes

- A6 says the 総務課長 "xem tất cả" and is the only one who moves a contract to 解約 (REQ-006). Whether the 総務課長 may also create contracts or change other items was not said: `[assumed]` no — index question 12.
