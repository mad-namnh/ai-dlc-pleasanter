## Why the one item failed

`references/interview.md` section 4 says a wish the KB does not cover "goes to the open questions for P2", and section 9 says "`[unverified]` wishes go to the open questions". The agent kept the `[unverified]` mark and the P2 item, and **also** wrote a requirement from the person's answer — for Teams (REQ-022) and in the same way for the five other wishes the KB does not cover: mail a number of days before a date (REQ-013…015), visibility per department (REQ-011), the 90-day lists (REQ-018, REQ-019), the total per 取引先 (REQ-020), refusing a 月額 that is not a whole number (REQ-017). The rubric names only Teams.

The skill text does not say whether "goes to the open questions" means *instead of* a requirement. Read strictly, the core of this request (the reminder mails) could not be a requirement either, because the KB has no entry on them. This is a gap of the skill text (and of what the rubric expects), not only of the run. **Nam decides** which rule holds; options in the report of 2026-11-11.

## Findings about the case itself

1. **The person-role script does not hold the idea.** The role-player rejected "hay bị quên gia hạn" in the summary as something she never said; the words are in her own first message (`input.md`), which `interviewee.md` does not contain. The agent handled it correctly (idea kept verbatim, correction recorded as A18, the words used nowhere else). The script needs the idea among its facts.
2. **The proposal path was not exercised.** The agent made no proposal, so "a proposal that was not accepted is not a requirement" was tested only through assumed defaults. The case cannot force a proposal; a second case, or a script in which the person asks "what do you suggest?", would.
3. **Relay mechanics.** A subagent's turn ends with a report to the Orchestrator, not with a chat message. The agent first reported itself blocked after round 1; from round 2 on the relay asked it to put the message for the person under "MESSAGE TO LAN:". For round 1 only the seven questions came back, without framing text. `evals/README.md` should say this.
4. **Role-player fidelity.** Every reply was checked against `interviewee.md`: no invented fact; what the script does not cover was answered with "không rõ, để chị hỏi lại".
