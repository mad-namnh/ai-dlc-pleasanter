# REQ-010 — 総務課長 views every contract

- **Status:** awaiting G1
- **Source:** `sources/2026-11-11-interview-vendor-contracts.md — A4`
- **Type (EARS):** ubiquitous

## Requirement

The system shall allow the 総務課長 to view every contract.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The 総務課長 opens the contract list and sees every contract, whatever its 担当部門 and status.
2. The 総務課長 opens any contract and its attached PDF.
3. Apart from setting 解約 (REQ-006), the 総務課長 does not edit contracts `[assumed]` (index #16).
